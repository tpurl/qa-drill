Q&A Drill

One pair per line, separated by a tab, | or ::. Copying two columns out of Excel works as-is. Lines with no separator are read as question, answer, question, answer… For spelling, put the word first and the definition second. You can add two optional columns for pronunciation: syllables and how it sounds, like necessary | needed | nec-es-sar-y | NESS-uh-sair-ee
